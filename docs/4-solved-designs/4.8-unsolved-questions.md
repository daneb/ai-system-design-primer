# 4.8 Additional design questions

Practice questions without full solutions, in the spirit of the original primer's additional
questions. Work each one through the method in [Part 1](../1-method/1-method.md): use cases and quality
bar, back-of-the-envelope, the lowest rung of the complexity ladder that works, components, hardening,
then scale. The middle column says what makes each one hard; the last column says where to start.

## Questions to ask yourself on any design

- Which leg of the lethal trifecta does it have, and which one does the design break?
  ([2.4](../2-trade-offs/2.4-capability-vs-security.md))
- What is the quality bar, and how is it measured before launch and in production?
  ([3.15](../3-components/3.15-evals.md))
- What happens when the model is wrong, slow, or unavailable?
- What does it cost per request at 10× the expected volume?
- Who can see what the system did, and for how long? ([3.16](../3-components/3.16-observability.md))

## Flashcards

Q: What is the first question to ask about any AI system design?
A: What is the quality bar, and how will it be measured before launch and in production?

Q: Name three questions to ask about any AI design before choosing components.
A: Which parts of the lethal trifecta it has; what happens when the model is wrong, slow or down; what it costs at ten times the expected volume.
