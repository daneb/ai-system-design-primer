# 5.2 Cloud mapping: Azure, AWS, Google Cloud

The primer pages name concepts, not products. This page does the opposite: for each capability in the
primer, which managed service or feature provides it on Azure, AWS and Google Cloud, at the two model
providers most used directly (OpenAI and Anthropic), and in open source.

## How to read it

- **Each row comes from a deep dive.** The link in the first column goes to the deep dive where the row
  was researched, with its sources. The tables below are generated from those deep dives by
  `python3 scripts/primer.py readme`, so they never drift from them.
- **"(not verified)"** means the deep dive's research didn't confirm the item from the provider's own
  documentation. Treat it as a lead, not a fact. **"—"** means no equivalent was found. **"·"** means the
  deep dive didn't cover that column.
- **Names change often** and many agent features are in preview. Check the provider's documentation
  before you design around a row; this page's `review_by` date is three months out for that reason.
- **A mapping isn't a recommendation.** Equivalent rows differ in limits, regions, pricing and maturity.
  The deep dives cover those differences.

## Picking a platform, in brief

- **Most enterprises use more than one.** A gateway in front of several providers
  ([3.1](../3-components/3.1-ai-gateway-and-router.md), [4.3](../4-solved-designs/4.3-enterprise-ai-gateway.md))
  keeps that manageable.
- **Data location and contracts usually decide first:** which regions, which retention and training
  terms, which providers your risk team has approved.
- **Then the platform you already run.** Identity, networking, logging and procurement are easier on the
  cloud you already operate.
- **Then model availability:** not every model is offered on every cloud, in every region, on every
  pricing tier.

## Flashcards

Q: Why does the primer keep product names out of its main pages?
A: Concepts last longer than product names; mappings to products live in the deep dives and this appendix, which are reviewed more often.

Q: What usually decides the choice of AI platform in an enterprise, before model quality?
A: Data location, contractual terms (retention, training) and provider approval; then the cloud the organisation already runs.

Q: What does "(not verified)" mean in the cloud mapping?
A: The item wasn't confirmed from the provider's own documentation; treat it as a lead and check before relying on it.
